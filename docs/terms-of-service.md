## What StoneHead is

StoneHead AI is a free web app with two sides:

- **the vibe** — an AI you can talk to about life, thoughts, whatever's on your mind. No age gate. Doesn't do strain recommendations, dosing, or grow diagnosis.
- **talk the plant** — cannabis cultivation help and strain information. **21+ only.**

It's built and run by one person (Michael Padilla). It is not a company, a licensed dispensary, a medical service, or a laboratory.

## What StoneHead is NOT

Read this part. It matters.

**StoneHead is not a doctor.** Nothing it says is medical advice. It doesn't diagnose conditions, prescribe anything, or tell you what's safe for your body. If you're having a medical emergency, call emergency services.

**StoneHead is not a therapist.** People talk to it about hard things, and I built it to be decent to them when they do. But it is not mental health care and it is not a crisis service. See [If you're in a bad place](#if-youre-in-a-bad-place) below — I've put more detail there than most apps do, because it matters more than most of what's in this document.

**StoneHead is not a lawyer.** Cannabis laws vary enormously by state and country. Nothing here is legal advice. Growing, possessing, and using cannabis is illegal in many places, including at the U.S. federal level. **You are responsible for knowing and following the law where you are.**

**StoneHead is not a lab.** It cannot analyze your plant, test your product, or confirm what anything is.

**StoneHead can be wrong.** It's an AI. It sometimes states things confidently that are inaccurate or entirely made up — including, I've watched it do this, talking about strains that don't exist as though it recognized the name. This is a known and unsolved limitation of AI systems generally, and it applies here. **Do not rely on it as your only source for anything that matters.** Cross-check grow advice. Verify anything before you act on it. If it tells you something and you can't confirm it elsewhere, treat it as unverified.

I'm not saying this to protect myself. I'm saying it because I've caught it doing exactly this and I'd rather you know.

## If you're in a bad place

StoneHead is not a crisis service and can't be one. It's a chat app run by one guy.

What it does do: when something you say suggests you might be in real trouble, it stops. It doesn't riff, it doesn't philosophize at you, and it doesn't agree with you about letting go of things when it isn't clear what you mean. It asks, plainly, and it points you toward people who are actually equipped for this — **988** in the US, call or text, any hour.

That behavior is built into the app itself rather than left to the AI's judgment in the moment, because the AI's judgment is exactly the thing I don't want to be relying on when it counts.

**It is a list of phrases, and lists miss things.** I've tested this against my own app and found it failing, more than once. It is better than nothing and it is not a safety net. If you are in danger, please contact 988 or emergency services directly rather than waiting to see what a chat app does.

**Nobody is watching in real time.** When the check fires, nothing is sent to me or to anyone else in the moment. No alert, no notification, no contact made on your behalf, ever. It's code, and a log I read later. If you need a person, you have to reach out to one.

**It sometimes fires when you didn't mean anything by it.** If you get a serious response out of nowhere, tell it what you actually meant and it'll drop it.

**You will never lose your account for telling StoneHead you're struggling.** Nothing in the rules below is meant to make you hesitate. If you're having a hard time, say so — that's a use of this app, not a violation of it.

## Drugs other than cannabis

StoneHead is a cannabis app. That's its lane, and it stays in it.

**It won't discuss other drugs' effects, or what they feel like, or how to obtain or make them.** Not because I think you can't handle it — because it isn't qualified, and an AI that makes things up confidently has no business describing what a substance will do to your body.

It will still talk about drug policy and history. That's a different thing from telling you what something feels like.

**One exception, and it's deliberate.** If you tell StoneHead you've taken something and you don't feel right, it will not answer "sorry, can't discuss that." It'll tell you to call 911, tell you what to say when you do, tell you that Good Samaritan laws in most states protect you from possession charges when you call for a medical emergency, and point you at naloxone if opioids might be involved.

That is not medical care and it is not an emergency service. **It is a chat app telling you to call the people who are.** If you might be overdosing, call 911 now rather than typing to an app.
